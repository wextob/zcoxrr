# Akku & Ali: Two Years in Our Little World (phone game)

Files: `index.html`, `config.js` (ALL dialogue and names), `game.js` (engine), `EDIT_DIALOGUE.md` (how to change what Ali says).

## Play
Built for phones (portrait or landscape): D-pad to move, big **A** button to talk, collect and play. The **Exit** button leaves any area. To try it on a computer, open `index.html` (keyboard: arrows/WASD, E or Space).
Tip: open the link on the phone, then use the browser menu > "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen app feel.

## Test checklist (do these yourself before sharing)
New game and intro skip; walk into each door; collect all 9 memories; Universe unlocks after the other four; ending plays; reload and check progress is saved; Pause > Restart wipes it; Journal replays memories; Dream Date games all finish; text looks sharp on your phone.

## Publish free (GitHub Pages)
1. Make a free GitHub account and a new public repository.
2. Upload `index.html`, `config.js`, `game.js`.
3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main` / root > Save.
4. After about a minute your link appears (usually `https://YOURNAME.github.io/REPONAME/`). Open it on your phone, then send it to Akku.

## Background music
Put your song in the `music/` folder as `bgm.mp3` (details in `music/README.txt`). Background music plays at 50% and sound effects at 100%; change both under `audio:` in `config.js`. Remember to upload the `music` folder to GitHub too.
